# Today

One day's tracks: per row `TrackBadge` (40dp, new `size` parameter; the card uses 32dp), name (one line, ellipsis, note icon straight after it), seven squares + streak in a `FlowRow` (the streak wraps under when it doesn't fit, 4dp below via `verticalArrangement`, the same gap as name to squares), and the Journal's `TickCell` at 48dp; the day's note as a third line under the name. **Spacing** (decision row 17): line 1 is as tall as the name, 4dp to line 2, 8dp from line 2 to the note line. Two things made it uneven in the first build: line 1's note icon was an `IconButton` (48dp of layout from `minimumInteractiveComponentSize`, fine in `TrackTitle`'s 64dp top bar, not here) and line 1 had `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`. The icon is now a 24dp clickable `Box` (role Button, the description on the box) around an 18dp `Icon`: Compose still gives a clickable under 48dp a 48dp touch area without taking layout space (`TodayUiTest` checks it with `assertTouchHeightIsEqualTo`). And the row is a small custom `Layout`: the cell (48dp) is taller than lines 1 + 2, so a `Row` put the note under the cell's bottom, ~2dp too low; the layout centres badge and cell on the line pair and hangs the note exactly 8dp under line 2. Rows capped at `MaxContentWidth`, start-aligned, `outlineVariant` divider.

- **Top bar**: the Journal's (`LargeTopAppBar`, small at compact height): title (`dayTitle`) and the line under it (`dayLine`), actions chip · spinner · ‹ · › (disabled on today) · ? (`HelpSheet` with `todayHelpParagraphs(notes)`, see `.claude/rules/journal.md`) · ⋮. The large bar was chosen over mock-up A's small one because at 360dp "Offline, unsaved changes" would leave no room for the date.
- **Pager by epoch day.** `HorizontalPager` with page index = `day.toEpochDay()` and `pageCount = today + 1`: left to right is old to new, so a swipe right goes back a day with no `reverseLayout` (RTL mirrors), and at midnight every page keeps its date. Settled page → `showDay`, **skipping the first value (`drop(1)`, do not remove)**: when Today comes back from the Journal its pager is restored on the page it was left on, and reporting that overwrote the day a Journal day label had just asked for (`TodayOpenDayTest`); `showDay` from elsewhere (arrows, chip, bar, Journal) → `animateScrollToPage`, or `scrollToPage` beyond 3 days. The title follows the view model's day, which changes only when a page settles, so it doesn't flicker mid-swipe. Tracks down needed `LazyRow` snapping instead of a pager because its pages were a few dp wide; full-screen pages are what `HorizontalPager` is for. `userScrollEnabled = false` while the card is open.
- **`TodayViewModel`**: the day shown, `fromJournal` and the note being written live in its `SavedStateHandle` (rotation, process restore; a fresh start shows today). Window `oldestLoaded..today`, starting at today − 29; `ensureLoaded(day)` extends it to 29 days before the oldest of `squareWindow(today, day − 1)` and `schedulePull`s only the new part. `refresh()` (ON_RESUME, pull to refresh): after midnight a Today on the old today moves to the new one; any other day stays. `openNote` only on an editable day with `notes`.
- **Squares** (`Squares` in `TodayRow.kt`, a `Canvas`): newest on the left (flipped by hand under RTL), the seven ending today while the day shown is among them, else the day shown and the six after it (`squareWindow`). Counter levels are the web's `levelFor` + `LEVEL_ALPHA` (`src/heatmap.ts`, ported line for line), drawn as `lerp(surfaceContainerHighest, squareColour, alpha)` with `maxValue` from `track_runs` (0 without stats); **a ticked yes/no square is level 2**, `lerp(empty, squareColour, 0.45)`, as on the web (decision row 13, changed by Martin on 29 Oct after seeing full colour on the phone; a track of an unknown type too, as `levelFor` treats it as yes/no). Level 2 is only about 1.9:1 against an empty square in light theme (1.7:1 for blue and yellow) and 2.7:1 in dark, under the 3:1 below: the web's choice, made by eye. The 3:1 test applies to the full strong colour, which counters reach at level 4, and `squareColour` is still what makes 45% visible at all (45% of a pale container would vanish). **`squareColour(colorKey, dark, primary)`** (`TodayText.kt`, decision row 19): the cell's pale container was ~1.0:1 against an empty square in light theme (a cell has its ✓ or number to tell it apart; a 10dp square has nothing else), so squares use a strong colour: `colorScheme.primary` with no custom colour (both themes, as the web's `fillFor`), a custom colour's `container` in dark theme, and the new `TrackColor.strong` (same hue, darker) in light theme. `TrackColorTest` checks every `strong` ≥ 3:1 against baseline light `E6E0E9` and every `container` ≥ 3:1 against baseline dark `36343B` (lowest: green 3.18, yellow 3.71). "Dark" is read from the scheme (`surfaceContainerHighest.luminance() < 0.5`), not the system, because App settings can force a theme. Empty squares, the outline and the journal's cells are unchanged. The day shown is outlined (1.5dp `outline`, 1dp out), dashed until ticked. Squares and streak are `clearAndSetSemantics` (the streak keeps a `todayRun` test tag) until the accessibility pass.
- **Notes** (with `notes`): the line-1 icon (`Description`, "Add a note for X") only on an open day without a note; a note line (icon, text, `maxLines = 3`, "Show more"/"Show less" when `hasVisualOverflow`) otherwise. On a locked day nothing opens the card: a tap on the name, the note or its icon shows the cell's locked-day toast instead (`LockedDayMessage`, shared with `TickCell`; Martin's change of 29 Oct, the spec had the tap do nothing). The line-1 icon stays hidden, and without `notes` the name does nothing. "Show more" still works.
- **The note card** (`NoteCardLayer`, over the whole screen, outside the Scaffold): a scrim that saves and closes on any tap (and swallows it), the card at the bottom with `ime ∪ navigationBars` padding, a `BackHandler` that saves and closes. **Enter is Done** (`ImeAction.Done`); two guards for keyboards that send a break anyway: `noteFieldChange` treats a change that only puts one `\n` at the selection as Done (pasted text keeps its breaks), and `onPreviewKeyEvent` makes hardware Enter save and Shift+Enter insert a break. Input goes through `cleanNoteInput` (CR/CRLF → LF, control characters except tab dropped, 500-code-point cap) as it arrives. **Closing the keyboard saves and closes** (`CloseWithKeyboard`: IME shown → hidden, only while RESUMED, because going home hides the keyboard too and the card must then stay); `ON_STOP` saves without closing. Check the guards on Gboard, Samsung Keyboard and a Bluetooth keyboard by hand.
